# Product Overview

This is a WhatsApp Business automation platform that enables businesses to manage customer conversations through AI-powered chatbots. The system provides:

## Core Features
- **WhatsApp Integration**: Connect and manage WhatsApp Business accounts
- **AI-Powered Conversations**: Automated customer interactions with configurable AI behavior
- **Product Catalog Management**: Manage business products with images and pricing
- **Conversation Training**: Train the AI with custom conversation examples
- **Dashboard Analytics**: Monitor conversation metrics and recent interactions

## Business Domain
The platform focuses on e-commerce and customer service automation, allowing businesses to:
- Automate customer support through WhatsApp
- Showcase and sell products via chat
- Train AI responses for better customer engagement
- Monitor conversation quality and AI confidence scores

## Target Users
Small to medium businesses looking to automate their WhatsApp customer interactions while maintaining quality customer service.
