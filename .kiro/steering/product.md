# Product Overview

This is a WhatsApp Business API automation platform that enables businesses to manage automated conversations with customers using AI. The system provides intelligent customer service, product recommendations, and conversation management through WhatsApp.

## Core Features

- **WhatsApp Business API Integration**: Connect and manage WhatsApp Business accounts with secure credential storage
- **AI-Powered Conversations**: LangChain-based AI system with configurable conversation goals (helpful, deal closer, support agent, lead generator)
- **Product Catalog Management**: Full CRUD operations for products with image upload, search, and filtering capabilities
- **Conversation Monitoring**: Real-time conversation tracking, confidence scoring, and manual escalation system
- **Training System**: AI training through conversation examples and configurable response styles

## Business Goals

The platform aims to automate customer interactions while maintaining quality through:
- Confidence-based escalation to human agents
- Customizable AI behavior patterns
- Comprehensive conversation analytics
- Manual review capabilities for quality control

## Target Users

Small to medium businesses looking to automate their WhatsApp customer service while maintaining personalized interactions and product sales capabilities.

## Localization

- **Primary Language**: Portuguese (Brazil)
- **AI Conversations**: All AI responses and training examples should be in Portuguese-BR
- **User Interface**: Frontend interface, labels, and messages in Portuguese-BR
- **Database Content**: Product descriptions, conversation examples, and system messages in Portuguese-BR
- **Cultural Context**: AI should understand Brazilian business culture, communication patterns, and local expressions
